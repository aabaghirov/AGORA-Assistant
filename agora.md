## About Agora
Agora is an education consultancy in Baku that helps students apply to schools and universities abroad. The website is available in Azerbaijani and English. The main next step for any family is an initial consultation, booked by phone or by form.

## Which program is right for me
Agora runs three programs by stage. START is for students from grade 5 onward. FORWARD is for grades 10-11 and covers university applications. RISE is for master's applicants. If someone is unsure, ask their grade or year, target countries, intended major and timeline.

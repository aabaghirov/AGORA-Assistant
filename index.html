From first question to booked consultation

Families get an answer in seconds. Agora's team gets a qualified lead with a summary.

  1. Ask anything. Programs, services, essays, process. Answers come only from Agora's approved content.
  2. Get matched. A few short questions: grade, countries, major, timeline. Then START, FORWARD or RISE.
  3. Book. Name, contact and preferred time, after a clear consent notice.
  4. Hand off. The team receives the lead by email and Google Sheet, with the conversation summarised.

What it does

Built to take pressure off the phone line, not to replace the consultants.

Grounded answers

Retrieval over Agora's own content. If it isn't sure, it says so instead of guessing.

Program recommender

Guided questions lead to START, FORWARD or RISE.

Lead capture

Consultation requests go to email and a Sheet or CRM row.

Human handoff

Pricing, guarantees, complex cases and frustrated users go to staff with context.

Bilingual

Azerbaijani and English, switchable in one tap, embedded on WordPress.

Analytics

Top questions, unanswered questions, drop-off and leads captured.

Guardrails

Many users are minors or parents, so the assistant is conservative by design.

No promises

Never promises admission or scholarships, never invents fees or deadlines.

Consent first

A short privacy notice appears before any contact details are collected.

Tested before launch

A 50-question test set reviewed by an Agora staff member.
